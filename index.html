Who We Are
2020
Year Founded

Founded in Jakarta, PT Goodyear Nusantara Energy was established by experienced oil & gas professionals to deliver world-class seismic and construction services to Indonesia's upstream sector.

We operate across the full project lifecycle — from seismic survey and data acquisition through mechanical pipeline installation and civil infrastructure construction — serving major KKKS including PHE ONWJ, BP Tangguh, Pertamina EP, INPEX Masela, ExxonMobil Cepu, and EDC E&P.

With regional offices in Jakarta, Balikpapan, and Batam, plus a strategic partnership office in Singapore, we deliver integrated project execution across all major Indonesian basins. Our in-house fleet and international technology partnerships enable competitive pricing, industry-leading TKDN compliance, and reliable delivery — on time, on budget, and to the highest international HSE standards.

Our Services

Core Capabilities

Three integrated service lines covering the full upstream oil & gas infrastructure value chain — from subsurface imaging to surface construction.

Seismic & Geophysics

Full-spectrum 2D and 3D seismic acquisition, topography survey, lane clearing, shot-hole drilling, and explosive/air gun recording — backed by modern data processing capabilities.

Mechanical Construction

Pipeline welding and installation, flowline construction, storage tank fabrication, metering and gas flare systems, CO₂ removal skids, and sandblasting/coating services.

Civil Construction

Wellpad construction for drilling and workover, land clearing and cutting, landfilling for site preparation, Central Processing Plant construction, and platform access facilities.

EPCI Delivery

End-to-end Engineering, Procurement, Construction & Installation capability — single-source accountability for complex projects from concept through commissioning.

TKDN & Local Content

Dedicated local content compliance team ensures every project maximizes Indonesian suppliers, labor, and materials — achieving TKDN targets of 50% and above across all contracts.

HSE & Quality

International HSE management system, ISO-certified processes, and a zero-lost-time-incident track record — safety built into every phase of every project.
