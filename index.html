About Us

Committed to Excellence in Oil & Gas Services

PT Goodyear Nusantara Energy is an established Indonesian oil and gas service company specializing in seismic exploration, mechanical construction, and civil construction. Our team brings decades of combined experience in the upstream sector.

Registered with SKK Migas, we are fully committed to providing high-quality services that meet international standards while adhering to all prevailing laws and regulations of the Republic of Indonesia.

"Our vision is to be a leading service company in the oil and gas industry through transparent operations, quality execution, and an unwavering commitment to safety in every project."
